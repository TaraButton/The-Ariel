Deadline 15 December 2026

Co-production and financing platform where up to 15 late-development feature documentaries pitch to international industry, with a cash innovation prize.

What you getPrize
Max per project€20,000
AmountNo grant for selection; one €20,000 Eurimages New Lab Innovation Award (restricted eligibility)
UK producersCan apply
Who can applyFeature projects over 60 minutes in late development, not yet in production; open worldwide; director has made at least one short; CHF 30 (by 2 Nov) or CHF 40 submission fee, CHF 360 participation fee if selected.
