Quick answers

When is the UK Global Screen Fund International Co-production deadline?

The next stated deadline is 24 November 2026.

How much does UK Global Screen Fund International Co-production give?

Up to £300,000 (non-recoupable); covers at most 50% of the overall UK financial contribution.

Who can apply to UK Global Screen Fund International Co-production?

Independent UK producers with demonstrable production experience; UK partner must be a minority co-producer; at least 60% of overall finance secured (80% for Fast Track), excluding the amount requested and AVEC, plus at least one other UK funding source; TV projects need two broadcasters (minority) or three (majority); requests above 25% of the UK contribution need strong justification.

How do I apply to UK Global Screen Fund International Co-production?

Apply through the funder's own page: https://www.bfi.org.uk/get-funding-support/funding-support-international-activity/uk-global-screen-fund/uk-global-screen-fund-ukgsf-international-co-production
