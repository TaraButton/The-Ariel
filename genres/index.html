Questions

Good to know

What arrives each morning?
Up to six new stories per genre: books, investigations and true stories with screen potential, each with a short note on why it could be a film or series and a link to the original. Rights-available titles from agents are marked with a gold star.
Do you check the rights?
Titles marked Rights available come to us directly from the literary agent, and we can introduce you. Everything else is a lead: we haven’t checked who holds the rights, and you can ask us to with a rights check.
What happens after 30 days?
You choose. Keep your genres for £29.99 a month (one genre) plus £10 for each extra, cancel any time. All eight come to £99.99, about the price of your own Ariel, so at that point ask us to build one around your slate: no setup fee, £75 a month at the founding rate. If you do nothing, the daily email simply stops.
Why are some genres “new”?
We only read and score a genre once someone has chosen it. Pick a new genre and we switch it on that day; your first stories arrive the next morning.
Can my colleagues see it?
Your link is personal, and it’s your key, so there’s no password. Each colleague can start their own free month, or ask about your own Ariel, where colleagues can be copied in.
