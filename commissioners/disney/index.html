Pitching to Disney+: quick answers

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at Disney+?

We list 5 commissioners at Disney+: Sean Doyle (Vice President, Unscripted, EMEA (Disney+)); Angela Jain (Head of Content, EMEA (Disney+)); Gaby Aung (Senior Manager, Unscripted (Commissioning), Disney+); Jon Swain (Commissioning Executive, Unscripted, Disney+); Tom McDonald (EVP of Content, National Geographic).

How do I pitch to Disney+?

The Walt Disney Company's policy is not to accept any unsolicited creative ideas or submissions. Approach through an established production company or agent. (source: thewaltdisneycompany.com).
