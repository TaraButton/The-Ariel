Sky commissioners: factual and documentary

Who commissions factual and documentary at Sky (UK), what they have commissioned recently and the official way to pitch. 5 commissioners, checked 7 November 2026.

All UK & US commissioners › Sky

Official pitch routes

  • Documentaries (Sky Documentaries / Sky Crime true crime / NOW): Email SkyFactual@Sky.uk via the 'Submit idea' button; no portal.
  • Entertainment (incl. talent-led and formatted factual entertainment): Email skyentertainmentideas@sky.uk via the 'Submit idea' button.
  • Arts (arts documentary, biography, music): Email ideas@sky.uk via the 'Submit idea' button.

What they say they want now

  • Sky Documentaries - what we're looking for · Bold, narrative-driven feature docs and limited series with scale (could become a scripted series or film); creative swagger, unrivalled access, UK resonance. Three focus areas: (1) personalities with UK fame and global recognition or astonishing life experiences; (2) true crime that takes you on a journey to the truth; (3) investigative thrillers retelling world-changing events with cinematic scale. NOT wanted: formatted or constructed docs, current affairs, presenter-led, specialist factual, anthology series. (undated; page live 2026-11-07 (footer (c) 2026))
  • Sky Entertainment - what we're looking for · Scalable formats and talent-led series with a strong hook that get people talking; not panel or quiz shows. (undated; page live 2026-11-07)

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at Sky?
