Official pitch routes

  • All (documentary, unscripted): Netflix does not accept or review unsolicited material. Pitches must come through a licensed agent, producer, attorney, manager or industry executive with an existing Netflix relationship.
  • Short documentary (emerging UK & Ireland filmmakers): Netflix Documentary Talent Fund (with Sheffield DocFest): teams pitch to a jury; five teams get £30,000 each for an 8-12 minute doc. 2026 cohort announced 10 Jun 2026; no application link in the article.

Pitching to Netflix: quick answers

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at Netflix?

We list 4 commissioners at Netflix: Adam Hawkins (Director of Documentary Series); Syeda Irtizaali (Director of Unscripted Series, Netflix UK); Kate Townsend (Director, Original Documentaries (title per 2021 sources; 2026 source says only 'Original Documentaries, Netflix')); Tom Barry (Documentary unit (exact title not given)).

How do I pitch to Netflix?

Netflix does not accept or review unsolicited material. Pitches must come through a licensed agent, producer, attorney, manager or industry executive with an existing Netflix relationship. (source: help.netflix.com).

What has Netflix commissioned recently?

Fred & Rose West: A British Horror Story (Adam Hawkins); Amy Bradley Is Missing (Adam Hawkins); Being Gordon Ramsay (Adam Hawkins).
