Channel 4 commissioners: factual and documentary

Who commissions factual and documentary at Channel 4 (UK), what they have commissioned recently and the official way to pitch. 26 commissioners, checked 7 November 2026.

All UK & US commissioners › Channel 4

Official pitch routes

  • All genres except News & Current Affairs and Sport: 4ideas portal for full proposals; initial top-line conversations by email to commissioners. General queries: 4producers@channel4.co.uk
  • 4ideas account sign-up / login: Register via 4ideas page
  • Documentaries & Factual Entertainment: Email top lines to the relevant Commissioning Editor first; when a full proposal is requested, submit via 4ideas (PACT-registered companies with relevant experience).
  • Specialist Factual: Email top lines / access or talent queries to a Commissioning Editor (cc assistant); full proposals with decks, scripts or sizzles via 4ideas.
  • News & Current Affairs (Dispatches, Untold, foreign, consumer): News & Current Affairs is excluded from 4ideas; make contact directly with the relevant commissioner using the contacts on the N&CA brief page. Unreported World is produced by ITN and not open to pitches.
  • Lifestyle / daytime / popular factual: Pitch specific ideas via a Commissioning Editor using the 4ideas portal; do not pitch the same idea to more than one Comm Ed.
  • More4: Contact the Lifestyle-team commissioners listed on the More4 brief; tariffs GBP50k-120k; everything must be repeatable on C4.

What they say they want now

  • All Current Briefs (index) · Rolling, ongoing pitches by genre; lists 11 genre brief pages. No dated cross-genre callouts were listed on 7 Nov 2026. (undated; checked 2026-11-07)
  • Documentaries & Factual Entertainment brief · Premium access docs, splashy single films, returnable precinct brands, formal innovation, ~1 factual drama a year, First Cut (2026 closed), daring social experiments, talent-led vehicles. (undated; checked 2026-11-07)
  • Specialist Factual brief · Revelatory singles with scoops, popular returners (history, science, adventure, travel, arts), provocative events/factual theatre; climate and AI priority in science; Secret History strand. (undated; checked 2026-11-07)
  • News & Current Affairs brief · Dispatches (plus in-house Investigations Unit), Untold youth strand (16-34, digital-first), foreign films, populist consumer programmes. (undated; text partly dated (refers to 2025))
  • Lifestyle brief · Lifestyle/daytime/features; property bucket full (not seeking property ideas except daytime brief); pitch via one Comm Ed on 4ideas. (undated; checked 2026-11-07)
  • More4 brief · Soft-doc 'intelligent comfort viewing' series; tentpoles, national-treasure-led, low-cost repeatable series; GBP50k-120k tariffs. (undated; checked 2026-11-07)
  • Unscripted Proof of Concept Fund · Development fund for big, original returnable unscripted formats ('poster test'); pitch via Docs & Fact Ent, Lifestyle, Reality & Ent or Specialist Factual through 4ideas, flagging the fund. No deadline stated. (Apr 2026)
