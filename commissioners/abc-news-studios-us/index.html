Pitching to ABC News Studios: quick answers

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at ABC News Studios?

We list 5 commissioners at ABC News Studios: Mike Kelley (Head of ABC News Studios); David Sloan (Senior Executive Producer, ABC News Studios); Beth Hoppe (Executive Producer, ABC News Studios); Katie Conway (Executive Producer, Robin Roberts unscripted development and production unit, ABC News Studios); Eamon McNiff (Executive Producer, ABC News Studios).

How do I pitch to ABC News Studios?

Disney Terms of Use s.7.A (covers Disney, ABC, ESPN, FX, National Geographic and other Disney brands): 'Our long-standing company policy does not allow us to accept or consider unsolicited creative ideas.' Pitch via agent/attorney or an established production company with an existing relationship. (source: disneytermsofuse.com).
