Pitching to Hulu: quick answers

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at Hulu?

We list 3 commissioners at Hulu: Rob Mills (EVP, Unscripted & Alternative Entertainment, Walt Disney Television); Jilly Pearce (SVP, Unscripted Programming (ABC and Hulu), Walt Disney Television); Belisa Balaban (SVP, Unscripted Series and Documentaries, Hulu Originals).

How do I pitch to Hulu?

Disney Terms of Use s.7.A (covers Disney, ABC, ESPN, FX, National Geographic and other Disney brands): 'Our long-standing company policy does not allow us to accept or consider unsolicited creative ideas.' Pitch via agent/attorney or an established production company with an existing relationship. (source: disneytermsofuse.com).
