Official pitch routes

  • Investigative / current affairs (FRONTLINE), science (NOVA), lifestyle (GBH Studio Six): GBH Content Submission Form (review GBH editorial guidelines and sign GBH Content Submission Release Form first). Rolling review; GBH says it will respond within 6-8 weeks if it wants to proceed. FRONTLINE prefers one-hour single-subject films and does not accept advocacy-funded projects; NOVA mainly wants 52-min docs but considers features and multipart series; sizzles/short proposals accepted.
  • Investigative documentary (FRONTLINE): FRONTLINE FAQ: well-researched proposals, completed films and works-in-progress via the GBH submissions portal; tips and story suggestions to frontline@pbs.org (about 500 suggestions/proposals a year).
  • Independent POV documentary (POV / POV Shorts): POV Call for Entries on FilmFreeway: completed films and fine cuts, shorts of 40 min or less, invited feature rough cuts. Deadline for the 2027 broadcast season was 31 Jul 2026; rolling submissions after that roll into the following year's cycle. Questions: cfe@pov.org.
  • Independent documentary funding (ITVS / Independent Lens): ITVS Get Funded page: Open Call (single nonfiction projects over 10 min already in production/post), Incubator Fund (early-stage features/web series for public media streaming) and Independent Lens Creator Lab (vertical micro-series) are all marked 'Not currently accepting applications' as of 8 Nov 2026. Series and Special Projects funding is invitation-only.
