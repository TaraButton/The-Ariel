Pitching to National Geographic (US): quick answers

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at National Geographic (US)?

We list 5 commissioners at National Geographic (US): Carolyn Bernstein (Executive Vice President, Documentary Films, National Geographic); Tim Horsburgh (Vice President, Documentary Films, National Geographic); Tom McDonald (Executive Vice President, Content, National Geographic); Janet Han Vissering (Senior Vice President, Development and Production, National Geographic Content); Charlie Parsons (Senior Vice President, Development, National Geographic).

How do I pitch to National Geographic (US)?

Disney Terms of Use s.7.A (covers Disney, ABC, ESPN, FX, National Geographic and other Disney brands): 'Our long-standing company policy does not allow us to accept or consider unsolicited creative ideas.' Pitch via agent/attorney or an established production company with an existing relationship. (source: disneytermsofuse.com).

Ghost Elephants (Carolyn Bernstein, Feb 2026); The Tale of Silyan (Carolyn Bernstein, Sep 2025); Ghost Elephants (Tim Horsburgh, Feb 2026); Best of the World with Antoni Porowski (Tom McDonald, Dec 2025); Best of the World with Antoni Porowski (Charlie Parsons, Dec 2025).
