UKTV commissioners: factual and documentary

Who commissions factual and documentary at UKTV (UK), what they have commissioned recently and the official way to pitch. 6 commissioners, checked 7 November 2026.

All UK & US commissioners › UKTV

Official pitch routes

  • All genres incl. factual, factual entertainment, true crime, docs: Email the idea to submissions@uktv.co.uk; proposals 1-2 pages; indies only (no individuals); include trigger-warning content notes in the email body per Film and TV Charity guidance; four-week response target. If no reply after four weeks, chase via niamh.claydon-mullins@uktv.co.uk (published on the commissioning page as the contact who flags ideas to the relevant team member). No commissioning rounds, no preferred-supplier list, no tariff.

What they say they want now

  • Factual & Factual Entertainment for U · Well-known talent immersed in worlds they're passionate about (incl. funny factual - Will & Ralf, Pete Wicks, Guy Martin, Robson Green, Dom Chinea); NOT social purpose, NOT reality. (undated; live 2026-11-07)
  • True crime for U · Wants U to be a true crime destination: well-known UK stories with name recognition, new way in/perspective, survivor point of view, treated sensitively; open to co-pros/co-commissions with international broadcasters; talent-led crime-adjacent projects and 'noisy' docs welcome; NOT blue-light formats (already has Special Ops: To Catch A Criminal). (undated; live 2026-11-07)
  • Cars / motoring for U&Yesterday · Next big repeatable, returnable classic-car format in new precincts with warm, relatable UK characters; nostalgia-led; closed episodes with a reveal plus a reason to return. (undated; live 2026-11-07)
  • Documentaries for U · Unmissable, statement docs with high-profile talent who bring authorship and vulnerability (e.g. Stacey Dooley Sleeps Over); single docs if editorially justified (e.g. Becoming Victoria Wood); NOT social purpose. (undated; live 2026-11-07)

Pitching to UKTV: quick answers

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at UKTV?

We list 6 commissioners at UKTV: Hilary Rosen (Director of Commissioning, UKTV); Helen Nightingale (Head of Factual and Factual Entertainment (Commissioning), UKTV); Emile Nawagamuwa (Commissioning Editor, UKTV); Kirsty Hanson (Senior Commissioning Editor, UKTV); Tracy-Jean (TJ) (Commissioning Executive, UKTV); Jason Dawson (Commissioning Executive, UKTV).

If no reply after four weeks, chase via niamh.claydon-mullins@uktv.co.uk (published on the commissioning page as the contact who flags ideas to the relevant team member). No commissioning rounds, no preferred-supplier list, no tariff. (source: corporate.uktv.co.uk).
