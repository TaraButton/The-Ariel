ITV commissioners: factual and documentary

Who commissions factual and documentary at ITV (UK), what they have commissioned recently and the official way to pitch. 6 commissioners, checked 7 November 2026.

All UK & US commissioners › ITV

Official pitch routes

  • Factual and Factual Entertainment (ITV1 9pm, ITVX docs/boxsets): Email a known commissioning editor or the departmental address factual.commissioning@itv.com with a killer title and 1-2 paragraphs. Sizzles not required.
  • Current Affairs (Exposure, Tonight, 9pm singles/short series): Email CurrentAffairs.Commissioning@ITV.com or tom.giles@itv.com. Production companies only.
  • Daytime (weekday afternoon formats, weekend morning lifestyle/cookery): Email Daytime.Commissioning@ITV.com. For ad-funded ideas, talk to the commissioning team and BE Studio before approaching brands.
  • Entertainment & Reality (incl. authored documentary with big talent): Email Entertainment.Commissioning@itv.com. Production companies only.
  • Sports documentary strand Outlier (diverse-led indies): Submission form via press release; applications closed 10 Apr 2026 (2026 round).
  • ITV2 factual / factual entertainment: No named route on the ITV2 page; it points to the main commissioning pages (factual address above).

What they say they want now

  • Factual Entertainment and Factual brief · Prime-time ITV1 9pm only (no pre-watershed). Premium, optimistic, big-hearted, broad appeal. Fact ent: ensemble talent, competition/constructed formats, event programming, authored ITVX shows with talent. Factual (ITVX): feature docs and doc boxsets with directorial flair; crime boxsets, anthologies, feature docs incl. cons and swindles; event and anniversary pieces. Wants exclusive access, surprising talent, witty titles. Avoid blues-and-twos, old-fashioned features, singing competitions, overly dark or dumbed-down ideas. (2025-11-19 (page shows 'Wednesday 19 November' with no year; 19 Nov fell on a Wednesday in 2025))
  • Current Affairs brief · Stories reflecting ITV's audience on social, political and economic issues; strong people-led narratives; access only where it drives narrative; mostly UK. Tonight: a few indie half-hours (core supply from ITV Studios/ITV News). Exposure: about seven investigative one-hour one-offs a year. Also 9pm singles or short (possibly two-part) series tied to events, exclusive interviews or reactive pieces. (2025-02-25 (page shows 'Tuesday 25 February' with no year; inferred 2025 from weekday))
  • Entertainment & Reality brief - Authored Documentary · Small number of authored documentaries with big talent telling new stories (example: Freddie Flintoff: Understanding Tourette's). Reality: fast-turnaround formats with a clear premise; no more dating or survival formats for now. (2026-10-27 (page shows 'Tuesday 27 October' with no year; inferred 2026 from weekday))
  • ITV2 brief · Factual must relate to younger audiences: true crime, wellbeing, celebrity. Fact ent expanded; returning series welcome, e.g. reality-familiar talent through life events, nightly location-based formats. Studio-based closed episodic formats less desirable. (2025-08-27 (page shows 'Wednesday 27 August' with no year; inferred 2025 from weekday))
  • Daytime brief · Weekday afternoons (2-6pm): quiz and formats, limited slots, talent helps. Weekend mornings: lifestyle and cookery, limited slots. Weekday mornings not commissioning. No factual-specific section. (2025-10-14 (page shows 'Tuesday 14 October' with no year; inferred 2025 from weekday))
  • Outlier - sports documentary strand (successor to Fresh Cuts) · Six standalone 46-min sports docs, GBP 50,000 each, for ITV's Year of Sport 2026; funded by Diversity Commissioning Spend; pitches only from companies owned by People of Colour and/or Deaf, Disabled and Neurodivergent creatives. Applications closed 10 April 2026. (Mar 2026)
