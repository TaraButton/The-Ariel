Who’s Buying What

20 November 2026 · Announcements 9 - 19 November 2026

Another lean fortnight for new factual orders. Channel 4 doubled the run of its Florida relocation series and added a third festive railway special, while Sky named the commissioners behind two new documentaries. ITVS has set the date for its next Independent Lens production call.

Get it every Friday: what UK and US broadcasters and streamers commissioned in documentary and factual, who moved, and which briefs opened.

What got commissioned 5

  • UK · Channel 4Escape to Florida (series 2)
    Recommission of the lifestyle series following Britons who move to Florida for a new life, rising from 20 to 30 episodes. Supported by Northern Ireland Screen.
    Commissioned by Jo Street (Director of Nations and Regions and Head of Lifestyle) and Jayne Stanger (Senior Commissioning Editor) · Made by Waddell Media
    Official announcement
  • UK · Channel 4Austrian Train At Christmas
    New 60-minute festive special riding the Südbahn line from Graz to Vienna over the Semmering Pass, narrated by Hugh Bonneville. The third in the run after Nordic and Alpine Train At Christmas; filming is under way.
    Commissioned by Jayne Stanger (Senior Commissioning Editor, Lifestyle) and Jo Street (Director of Commissioning, Nations & Regions and Head of Lifestyle) · Made by King of Sunshine Productions with WPP Media Motion Entertainment
    Official announcement
  • UK · Channel 4 (YouTube)I Exposed Newcastle's Hidden Camera Creators
    Digital documentary in the In Too Deep strand in which Tommy Murphy looks into creators who secretly film people on Newcastle's streets for social media. Backed by North East Screen; out now on Channel 4's YouTube channel.
    Commissioned by Ellie Winstanley (Commissioning Editor, News and Current Affairs) · Made by Rock Paper Productions
    Official announcement
  • UK · Sky DocumentariesDon't Forget Me: The Disappearance of Amy Fitzpatrick
    Three-part series (3x60) on the 2008 disappearance of 15-year-old Amy Fitzpatrick in Spain, with access to both sides of her divided family and the Irish police. On Sky and NOW from 29 November.
    Commissioned by Phil Edgar-Jones (Executive Director, Unscripted Originals), Hayley Reynolds (Head of Documentary Commissioning) and Kathryn Taylor (Commissioning Editor) · Made by The Slate Works
    Official announcement
  • UK · Sky ArtsDangerous Debussy
    One-hour film on Claude Debussy's private life and the women around him, narrated by Bessie Carter with performances of his music. Premieres 1 December on Sky Arts and NOW.
    Commissioned by Phil Edgar-Jones (Executive Director, Unscripted) and Benedetta Pinelli (Commissioning Editor) · Made by Section 52 Films
    Official announcement
