Who’s Buying What

8 November 2026 · Announcements 28 October - 8 November 2026

A quiet week for factual orders, with the UK public broadcasters supplying almost all of it: renewals for proven formats, a BBC Arts history series and a digital spin-off of BBC Scotland's Murder Trial. Channel 4 also confirmed Married at First Sight UK will not return, saying the budget will go into new programmes.

Get it every Friday: what UK and US broadcasters and streamers commissioned in documentary and factual, who moved, and which briefs opened.

What got commissioned 5

  • UK · Channel 4The Hormone Fix
    One-off 60-minute factual special in which Davinia Taylor looks at how hormones shape sleep, mood, stress and energy, with experts and contributors. Due on Channel 4 next year.
    Commissioned by Clemency Green (Senior Commissioning Editor) and Jo Street (Director of Commissioning, Nations & Regions) · Made by Little Bird Films
    Official announcement
  • UK · Channel 4 (More4)Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids (series 5 and 6)
    Double-series renewal for More4's top-rated show, following the Owen family as they restore a derelict Swaledale farmhouse alongside farm life at Ravenseat.
    Commissioned by Jayne Stanger (Commissioning Editor) · Made by Wise Owl Films
    Official announcement
  • UK · Sky DocumentariesThe Fans Who Fought Back: The Battle for Blackpool
    Sky Original feature doc (1x60) on Blackpool FC's rise to the Premier League and the long supporter campaign against the Oyston family. Directed by James Newton; airs 21 November.
    Commissioned by Phil Edgar-Jones (Executive Director, Unscripted Originals); Commissioning Editor Jo Hughes · Made by South Shore Productions
    Official announcement
  • UK · BBC Two (BBC Arts)Beethoven: Rise of a Genius
    Three-part drama-documentary for the 200th anniversary of Beethoven's death, drawing on his letters, immersive sound design and Deaf contributors. Co-production with ZDF and PBS.
    Commissioned by Suzy Klein (Head of BBC Arts and Classical Music TV); Commissioning Editor Mark Bell · Made by 72 Films
    Official announcement
  • UK · BBC ScotlandInside Murder Trial: Death of a Dog Walker
    Nine-part video podcast for iPlayer and YouTube extending the Murder Trial strand, with a journalist and a defence KC analysing each day of the Aberfeldy murder trial.
    Commissioned by Heather Kane-Darling (Commissioning Executive, BBC Scotland) · Made by Firecrest Films
    Official announcement
