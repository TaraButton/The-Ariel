Official pitch routes

  • Production companies (proposals): Discovery Producer's Portal: register, then submit proposals and track them. Still online (checked 7 Nov 2026) but branded Discovery Communications with a 2018 copyright, so confirm with the UK team that it is still used for WBD UK & Ireland.
  • All unscripted / factual (UK & Ireland): No public UK pitch portal or published commissioning inbox found. WBD UK & Ireland announces commissions via its GB press site, where releases credit the commissioner (Charlotte Reid) and the exec who ordered the series (Graham Lafferty). Approach via the named commissioning team.
  • Emerging / diverse producers (talent schemes): WBD Access runs programmes via Submittable; on 2026-11-07 the page said 'There are presently no open calls for submissions.' A WBD Access Production Incubator for UK food/cooking concepts (emerging and mid-level prodcos) was announced 31 Mar 2026; details are Pact-members-only.
