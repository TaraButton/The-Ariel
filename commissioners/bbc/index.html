Who commissions factual and documentary at BBC (UK), what they have commissioned recently and the official way to pitch. 42 commissioners, checked 7 November 2026.

Official pitch routes

  • All network TV factual genres (documentaries, history, science, natural history, religion & ethics, arts & classical music, current affairs incl. Panorama, popular music, factual entertainment, BBC Three): PiCoS ideas submission system - all ideas must be submitted via PiCoS to the named genre commissioners; for UK production companies with a qualifying commission in the last 3 years (BBC Pitch is closed)
  • PiCoS login: PiCoS portal
  • PiCoS technical/registration support: Published support address picos.support@bbc.co.uk (system support only, not for pitching)
  • Storyville (international feature docs and shorts): Storyville online submission system
  • Factual acquisitions (completed, funded films only): Factual acquisition online submission system; BBC says it cannot track submissions sent to individual email addresses
  • Supply/business questions (smaller or out-of-London indies) - not content: Contact Lucy Willmore, Commissioning Supply Executive, via contact form
  • Individuals / non-indies: Skills and training pages (individuals cannot use PiCoS)

What they say they want now

  • Documentary commissioning brief · Narrative/character-driven docs; returnable mainstream access series (4-10 eps) and 'next precinct beyond blue flashing lights'; limited series/box sets; high-impact single films (60 min or feature); BBC Three 30-45 min box sets; new No Filter (w/t) iPlayer strand for younger audiences, topical, 15-40 min forms; nations portrayal; new directors; co-pros welcome (live 2026-11-07 (page built 2 Nov 2026))
  • Current affairs commissioning brief · Panorama: ambitious investigative revelation (pitch to Adam Grimley via PiCoS); BBC Two/iPlayer series and singles with strong title/image and news-making potential, YouTube spin-offs; BBC Three single-narrative stories from last five years with original journalism for 16-34s (live 2026-11-07)
  • Factual homepage (Director of Factual statement) · All pitches must have iPlayer baked in, with a strong title and thumbnail vision; ideas taken from indies at any time (live 2026-11-07)
  • History / Science / Natural history / Religion and ethics briefs · Specialist factual genre briefs with team lists; all via PiCoS (live 2026-11-07)
  • Arts and classical music brief · First points of contact: Mark Bell (singles BBC One/Two, landmarks), Alistair Pegg (box sets/formats/limited series BBC Two), Stephen James-Yeoman (classical music, Arts Digital), David Harron (Scotland), Julian Carey (Wales) (live 2026-11-07)
  • Storyville expands to premium shorts · Storyville seeking 10-40 minute short documentaries for BBC Four and iPlayer exploring issues shaping the world; new talent encouraged (Jun 2026)
  • New pan-UK commissioning model · Nations commissioners (NI, Scotland, Wales) responsible for ideas with strong on-screen nations portrayal and are key contact for indies in their nation; joint sign-off with genre head; aim for titles made in the place they portray (Jul 2026)
  • YouTube deepwatch channel brief · 20 standalone YouTube-first docs (min 25 min, GBP 50k tariff) for 16-24s, from nations/English-regions indies; deadline 7 May 2026 (closed) (Apr 2026)
  • New Entertainment commissioning structure · Factual Entertainment & Events genre to close in autumn 2026; replaced by Studio Entertainment, Constructed Reality, and Features and Events under Ed Havard (Jun 2026)

Pitching to BBC: quick answers

Who commissions documentaries and factual programmes at BBC?

We list 42 commissioners at BBC: Fiona Campbell (Director of Factual); Poppy Dixon (Head of Documentaries); Emma Loach (Lead Commissioning Editor, Documentaries); Beejal-Maya Patel (Commissioning Editor, Documentaries); Carl Callam (Commissioning Editor, Documentaries); Anna Dickeson (Commissioning Editor, Documentaries); and more below.

How do I pitch to BBC?

PiCoS ideas submission system - all ideas must be submitted via PiCoS to the named genre commissioners; for UK production companies with a qualifying commission in the last 3 years (BBC Pitch is closed) (source: bbc.co.uk).

What has BBC commissioned recently?

New BBC documentaries slate (Emergency 24/7, Murder 24/7 etc.) (Fiona Campbell, Jan 2026); Murder Trial (two new cases) (Emma Loach, Jul 2026); Bel (Emma Loach, Jun 2026); Emergency 24/7 (Emma Loach, Jan 2026); Stacey & Joe series 3 (Beejal-Maya Patel, Apr 2026).
