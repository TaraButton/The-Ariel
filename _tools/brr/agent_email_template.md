# Agent email template - British Review of Reviews (v1, 2026-11-02)

From: Tara Button <tara@theariel.tv>. One email per agency per edition. Plain, short, warm. HTML body
with every link under descriptive text (never a bare URL). No em dashes.

**Subject (one title):** <Book> in this week's British Review of Reviews
**Subject (several):** <Agency> titles in this week's British Review of Reviews

---

Dear <first name>,

I'm Tara Button, founder of The Ariel, which helps film and TV producers find stories early. Every Monday
we publish the [British Review of Reviews](https://theariel.tv/reviews/), a free round-up of every
non-fiction book reviewed in the British papers, and <Author>'s <Book> is in this week's edition after
its review in <publication>.

[OPENING - choose by what is TRUE in the Requests tab / `op: demand` for this book:]
- **A producer has asked** (a Check Rights request from someone whose role is Producer, Development
  executive or Commissioner, or who is an Ariel member or Rights Pass holder): make this the FIRST line
  after the greeting, before the introduction:
  "Our producers have asked us to find out the film rights status for <Book>." (Tara's wording, 2 Nov.)
  With more than one: "Two of our producers have asked..." Use the real count. Then the introduction
  paragraph as normal.
- **Only other readers asked** (role blank, Writer, Agent, Other): "Readers of the British Review of
  Reviews have asked us to find out the film rights status for <Book>." Never call them producers.
- **Nobody has asked yet, but it was in the edition's top 10 by review clicks:** add "It was one of the
  most-read reviews in this week's edition." after the introduction.
- **No data:** no demand line at all.

Could you tell me whether the film and TV rights are available, or who handles them if that's a
colleague or co-agent? One line back is plenty. If they are available, may we show our readers that
they are? We'd only ever share your details when a producer asks for an introduction.

If you'd like producers on The Ariel to hear about other titles with rights available, you're welcome to
send them to rights@theariel.tv at any time. It's free for agencies, and a line and a link is all we need.

With best wishes,
Tara

Tara Button
The Ariel | [theariel.tv](https://theariel.tv)

**Several titles from one agency:** replace the second sentence with a short list:
"... and these titles of yours are in this week's edition:" then one line each: <Book> by <Author>
(reviewed in <publication>). Ask about rights "for any of these".

**Existing relationship** (agency already Active in the Agent Outreach tracker or already sends to
rights@): drop the introduction and the rights@ paragraph; open with "Lovely to see <Book> in this
week's British Review of Reviews."

**Never:** praise or judge the book, say what commissioners want, ask for decks/excerpts/pitches, promise
follow-up work Tara hasn't agreed, or claim partners/clients we don't have.
